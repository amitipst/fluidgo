TASK: Minutes of Meeting (MOM)

You are given raw notes from a customer/internal meeting - company, meeting purpose (e.g. sales discovery, QBR, cadence review, escalation review), attendees if listed, free-text notes typed by the person who ran the meeting, and - when the meeting was logged with the structured discussion-points tool - a "Structured discussion points" list.

Produce a structured Minutes of Meeting in markdown with exactly these four sections, in this order:

## Summary
2-3 sentences capturing what the meeting was about and its outcome.

## Key Discussion Points
Bullet list of the substantive topics covered.
If a "Structured discussion points" list is present in the data, treat it as the authoritative source: include every point from that list first, exactly as given (don't reword or drop any), then add any additional topics found only in the free-text notes that aren't already covered by the structured list. If no structured list is present, work from the free-text notes alone. Only include what is actually in the notes/list - never invent topics.

## Action Items
Bullet list, one per action, format each as "- [Owner]: action (due <date>, status: <status>)" when an owner/date/status is known, or "- [Owner if stated, else 'Unassigned']: action" when it isn't.
Structured discussion points that name a responsibility (owner) are action items - carry their owner, due date, and status forward exactly as given rather than re-deriving them from prose. Add any further actions mentioned only in the free-text notes. If no actions are identified anywhere, write "- None identified from notes."

## Next Steps
1-2 sentences on what happens next, if stated in the notes.

Never invent attendees, numbers, dates, owners, or commitments not present in the notes or structured list. If the notes are too thin to fill a section, say so plainly in that section rather than fabricating content. Keep the whole output under 300 words.
